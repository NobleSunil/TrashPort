About TrashPort

Transforming Urban Waste Management Through Smart Logistics

TrashPort is an end-to-end, on-demand smart waste management and recycling platform designed to modernize urban sanitation. By bridging the gap between households, field collectors, and municipal authorities, we eliminate the inefficiencies of traditional static route scheduling. Our platform optimizes waste logistics through real-time pickup requests, AI-assisted customer service, and distance-based automated dispatching.

Our Mission

We aim to build a cleaner, more sustainable urban ecosystem by making waste collection transparent, efficient, and rewarding. We believe that by incentivizing citizens to recycle and empowering collectors with fair, automated earnings, we can revolutionize how cities handle their daily waste footprint.

How the Ecosystem Works

Designed for seamless collaboration between citizens, collectors, and city administrators.

For Citizens

On-Demand & Rewarding

Schedule waste collection on your terms. Citizens can easily book pickups, categorize their waste, and estimate weight—all assisted by our integrated Groq AI chatbot. Through our digital wallet system, users earn recycling incentive points for high-value categories, while active subscribers enjoy zero-fee routine collections.

For Collectors

Optimized & Fair Routing

We empower verified drivers with a live, geolocation-aware job board. Our automated dispatch engine uses proximity algorithms to assign the nearest available jobs. With on-site weight verification and automated commission calculations—factoring in both base fees and travel distance—collectors are guaranteed transparent and immediate digital payouts.

For Administrators

Complete Operational Oversight

The Admin Control Room is the central nervous system of TrashPort. City authorities and platform managers gain real-time visibility through live GPS fleet tracking, automated compliance audits, and dynamic dashboard analytics covering revenue, recycled tonnage, and collector payouts.

Powered by Modern Technology

Scalable, fast, and secure infrastructure designed for modern civic operations.

TrashPort is built for scale and speed. Powered by a robust Django framework, REST APIs, and PostgreSQL, the platform integrates Stripe for seamless payments and Haversine distance calculations for precision routing. It is a comprehensive technological solution for a complex civic challenge.
